My favorite beverage lately has been a latte. I like it because a well made latte in my opinion can have really great flavors of the coffee bean in it as well as the milk adds a bit of sweetness to it so it's not too bitter. My latte uses coffee beans from Bird Rock which contain flavor notes of black pepper, red apple, and fudge. I usually prepare it at home with my espresso machine. Ideally, I like to make and drink it in the morning but if I'm busy in the morning I'll have one in the evening. I would definately recommend it to anyone who likes coffee but if they don't then most likely not.
