Our Heritage & Vision

Pacific Trails Resort opened in 2012 when founders Mark and Elena Vance bought a 40-acre parcel along the Big Sur coast. Originally built as a small base camp with four canvas tents and a single communal fire pit, the property was designed for hikers exploring the surrounding Big Sur state parks. Over the past decade, the resort expanded into ten insulated yurts equipped with redwood decking, solar-powered heating, and private ocean-view balconies.

Sustainability remains built into daily operations rather than treated as a marketing tagline. The resort draws 100% of its electricity from a 15-kilowatt on-site solar array and rainwater harvesting tanks that supply non-potable water for irrigation. Through a formal partnership with the California State Parks System, a portion of every booking directly funds local trail maintenance along the Central Coast.

Our Team

Our team consists of eight full-time staff members and licensed local guides with years of field experience in coastal ecology, wilderness first aid, and marine navigation. Instead of generic itineraries, our guides run daily scheduled activities which include 6:00 AM coastal bird walks along the ridge trails and low-tide marine life tours at the coves, or we can meet with you individually to map out routes based on your mileage, terrain preferences, and weather conditions.
